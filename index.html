How to use the Idea Board

Starting a collection

Click "+ New idea collection" to create a parent box (red). Once it exists, it shows a "+ idea" button and a "delete" button.

Adding ideas

Click "+ idea" on any box to create a connected child box (white/green). Child boxes can also have their own "+ idea" children, so you can branch as deep as you like.

Viewing and editing a box

Click any box (when link mode is off) to open its details on the right: Title, an optional Thumbnail image URL, a Summary, and a Link field for pointing to a full page, file, or site. Edits save automatically.

Previewing without clicking

Hover your mouse over any box to see a tooltip with its title and full summary — handy for a quick peek without opening the side panel.

Linking two existing boxes

Click "Link boxes" to turn on link mode. Click one box, then click the box you want to connect it to — a new wire draws between them. Click "Link boxes" again to turn link mode off.

Unlinking boxes

Make sure link mode is off. Click on the linked box — under its title in the right-side panel, you'll see a "Linked from" row listing every box it's connected to as a small tag. Click the × on a tag to remove that one connection.

Finding a box in a big board

Click "Toggle list" to open a left-hand list of every box on the board. Click any entry to jump straight to it on the canvas.

Saving your work

The board auto-saves in your browser as you go. Click "Save to file" anytime to download a backup copy as a file. Click "Load from file" to bring a saved file back in (this replaces what's currently on the board).

Deleting a box

Click "delete" on a box. If any of its linked boxes would be left with no other connection, you'll be asked to confirm — those get removed too. A box that still has another link stays put, just missing that one connection.

Changing the look

Click "Theme" to pick a preset background (Nature, Space, Underwater, Medieval, Dungeons & Dragons) or upload your own custom background image.

Background music

Click "Music" to either upload an audio file to play while you work, or paste a YouTube link to play that in the background instead.
